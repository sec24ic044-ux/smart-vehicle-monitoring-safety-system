The Problem

Why Vehicles Need
Smart Monitoring

Road accidents and unexpected breakdowns are rising. The root causes are preventable — if only vehicle owners had the right data in time.

💥
Rising Road Accidents

Road accidents continue to surge globally, largely driven by overspeeding and lack of real-time vehicle condition awareness during critical moments.

🔧
Unexpected Breakdowns

Poor preventive maintenance leads to sudden failures on the road — engine problems, dead batteries, and empty fuel tanks that could have been predicted.

📵
No Live Vehicle Data

Parents and vehicle owners have zero access to live vehicle health data, leaving them unaware of dangerous conditions or risky driving behaviour.

⏳
Delayed Emergency Response

When accidents happen, the delay in notifying emergency services and family members can mean the difference between life and death.

The Solution

A Connected, Intelligent
Safety Platform

Our IoT system gives real-time visibility into every critical vehicle parameter — and acts instantly when it matters most.

⛽
Fuel Level Tracking

Continuous fuel monitoring with low-level alerts prevents unexpected stops and enables smart refuelling planning.

🔋
Battery Condition

Real-time battery health analytics detect degradation early, preventing unexpected power failures on the road.

🔩
Engine Health

IoT sensors monitor engine temperature, RPM, and performance metrics to flag issues before they escalate into costly repairs.

📡
Speed Monitoring

Live speed tracking with configurable overspeed alerts promotes safe driving habits and compliance with speed limits.

🚨
Automatic SOS Alerts

On accident detection, the system instantly dispatches SOS alerts to ambulance, police, and family members — with GPS location — in under 2 seconds.

📲
Remote Visibility

Parents and fleet owners access live dashboards from anywhere — full visibility into vehicle health and driving behaviour at all times.
